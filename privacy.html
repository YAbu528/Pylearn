YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy describes the current PyLearn website and its account, learning-progress, and browser-storage features. As the product changes, this policy may be updated. Please check the effective date above.

1. Information PyLearn handles

Depending on how you use the site, PyLearn may handle:

  • Account and sign-in information: your email address and authentication/session information managed through Supabase and, if selected, an identity provider such as Google, Microsoft, or GitHub.
  • Learning information: lesson completion, quiz results, challenge attempts, XP and streak statistics, achievements, bookmarks, lesson reviews, and notes you choose to save.
  • Preferences and drafts on your device: interface preferences and unfinished project drafts may be stored in your browser's local storage. Preview and production site data use separate storage keys.

PyLearn does not ask you to enter payment-card details to use the learning features described here. Do not put passwords, private keys, or other sensitive information into lesson notes or code examples.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate your account, save and synchronize learning progress, display your dashboard and achievements, remember preferences, and operate and improve the learning experience.

3. Where information is processed

Account authentication and cloud-synchronized learning records are handled using Supabase. When you choose a social sign-in option, the selected provider participates in authentication and applies its own privacy terms. The site also loads software libraries and learning-runtime resources from third-party content-delivery services. Those services may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser request metadata, when your browser connects to them.

PyLearn is hosted as a static website through GitHub Pages. GitHub, Supabase, authentication providers, and content-delivery services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Review their policies before using those services.

4. Browser storage

PyLearn uses browser local storage for certain preferences and unfinished drafts. Supabase Auth manages the sign-in session in browser storage. Clearing browser data can remove locally stored preferences or drafts and may sign you out. Cloud-synchronized records are separate from those local items.

5. Sharing

PyLearn does not sell personal information. Information is shared with the service providers described above only as needed to host the site, authenticate accounts, deliver site resources, and provide the features you use. Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or to protect the security of the service and its users.

6. Retention and your choices

Cloud records are retained in the connected service while they are needed to operate your account and learning history. Local preferences and drafts remain in your browser until you clear them or the browser removes them. Signing out ends your active session but does not by itself delete cloud records. PyLearn currently does not provide a self-service account-deletion control. For a privacy or deletion request, use the PyLearn project support page; do not post personal or account-sensitive information publicly.

7. Children's privacy

PyLearn is an educational website. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information associated with a child's account, contact the project maintainers through the support page above and avoid sharing private details in a public post.

8. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used by the services that operate PyLearn, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password and keep your sign-in credentials private.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as features or service providers change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the PyLearn project support page. Avoid including passwords, private notes, or other sensitive account information in public reports.
